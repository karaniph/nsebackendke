The Nairobi Securities Exchange posted modest gains on July 3 as liquidity inched higher and a few cash-generative names drew interest. The NASI rose 0.57 percent to 225.98, and the NSE 20 gained 0.78 percent to 3,809.79, signaling a cautious but constructive tone. Market activity centered on large-cap counters that offer stable dividend streams, helping lift sentiment despite outsized foreign selling. Total turnover clocked in at 799.42 million shillings, with the overall market capitalization nudging higher to 3,792.33 Bn.

FOREIGN FLOW
Foreign flow remained negative on the day, with a net outflow of 131.32 million shillings.
The standout inflow was Safaricom at 21.62 million, while Equity Group accounted for the bulk of selling at 129.20 million.
Overall foreign purchases totaled 166.12 million against foreign sales of 297.44 million, underscoring a risk-off tilt that persisted despite strength in select stocks.
Investors were also weighing Safaricom's dividend and related timelines, with the stock trading near 34.05 shillings as ex-dividend considerations loomed.
