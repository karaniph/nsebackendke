## Market Pulse

The Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) concluded trading on **19 May 2026** with a lack of official index-level data, as neither the **NASI** nor the **NSE 20** figures were reported in the monitored close cards. Despite this, market participation remained active, with broker notes and market chatter indicating robust engagement in select counters. Liquidity conditions were described as healthy, though traders were cautioned to focus on stocks with confirmed volume at the close to avoid potential reversals in lower-liquidity names.

Foreign money flow details were notably absent from the official close cards, leaving market participants reliant on broker wraps for net prints. The **Central Bank of Kenya (CBK)** maintained its benchmark rate at **8.75%**, while the **91-day T-bill yield** held steady at **8.318%**, signaling stability in fixed-income markets. Global macroeconomic factors, including **US rate expectations**, oil price fluctuations, and broader risk sentiment, continued to influence frontier equity flows. However, local drivers such as **CBK policy decisions**, inflation trends, and **foreign exchange (FX) stability** remained the primary determinants of market direction.

## What Moved

• **Blue Chips in Focus**: Broker watchlists highlighted **SCOM**, **EQTY**, and **KCB** as key stocks under observation. Financials and consumer staples led sector performance, with these names drawing attention for their momentum and liquidity. While no official top gainers or losers were reported, market dispersion suggested selective strength in liquid counters, reinforcing the importance of volume confirmation.

• **Fixed Income Stability**: The **CBK’s** monetary policy stance remained unchanged, with the **91-day T-bill** yielding **8.318%**. Secondary-market bond activity was subdued, as traders awaited the next auction for clearer directional cues. The absence of updates on the **182-day** and **364-day T-bill yields** left market participants reliant on upcoming **CBK** operations for further insights.

• **Technical Caution**: Broker notes emphasized the risk of reversals in lower-liquidity stocks following sharp one-day moves. Traders were advised to prioritize volume confirmation before making entry decisions, particularly in names with volatile price action.

• **Dividend Quiet**: No dividend announcements or updates were reported, marking a period of relative inactivity in corporate actions. This lull suggested that investors were awaiting further developments before making dividend-driven decisions.

## Risks

• **Liquidity Fragmentation**: The absence of index-level data posed challenges for traders attempting to assess broad market health. Lower-liquidity stocks, in particular, carried heightened reversal risks, especially after volatile sessions where volume confirmation was lacking.

• **Global Macro Pressures**: Frontier markets, including the **NSE**, remained sensitive to external factors such as **US rate expectations**, oil price swings, and shifts in global risk sentiment.
