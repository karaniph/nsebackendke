## Common Mistakes
- Treating a single metric as a complete investment thesis.
- Applying international benchmarks without adjusting for Kenyan market conditions.
- Ignoring liquidity constraints when sizing positions on smaller counters.

## Checklist
- Identify the metric's formula and what it measures before applying it.
- Cross-check against sector peers for context.
- Define your invalidation point before building any position.
