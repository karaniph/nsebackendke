- NASI All-Share climbed 0.61% to 240.501, signaling a gentle bid across large-cap and mid-cap names. Volume and turnover printed at 512,515,489.19 shillings and 512,515,489.19 shares, respectively, underscoring a cautious but active session.

Gainers were mostly within financials, with no large surprise driver released in public notes. The lack of a disclosed flow signal makes the move more technical and liquidity-driven than news-driven.

## Sector & themes
