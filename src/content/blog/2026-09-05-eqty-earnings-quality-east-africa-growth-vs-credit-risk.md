## Valuation lens  — how to think about value here; anchor on earnings durability, not a single multiple.

Value in **EQTY** should hinge on earnings durability rather than a single multiple. In a region where credit risk can shift quickly, the right lens is:
- How resilient is the earnings base when loan growth accelerates or slows? Durable earnings come from a mix of stable NIMs, predictable fee income, and low-provisioning surprises.
- What does the provisioning trajectory imply about future charge-offs? A run-rate of prudent reserves with stable utilization signals quality growth.
- How diversified is **EQTY**’s regional book? A broader East Africa footprint reduces concentration risk but introduces FX and regulatory considerations.
- Do operating costs scale with revenue, maintaining a healthy cost-to-income ratio? Balance-sheet growth must be translated into sustainable profitability.

Given the 90-day signal, investors should anchor valuation on the trajectory of loan growth balanced by prudent provisioning, rather than chasing a near-term earnings beat. A durable earnings narrative will emerge if **EQTY** can maintain margins while expanding in higher-quality segments and keeping non-performing exposure in check.
