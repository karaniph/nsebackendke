- Governance and succession risk in a complex multinational bank structure can impact strategic execution and payout discipline.

## Rates & liquidity context
Liquidity conditions and the cost of funds are the primary swing factors for banks in this market. When liquidity is tight, banks must price deposits more aggressively and manage credit risk more cautiously, which can squeeze margins. Conversely, an environment of ample liquidity can pressure pricing and compress net interest margins, even as transaction volumes rise. Central bank policy settings and routine liquidity management operations shape the bank’s funding mix and the stability of non-interest income that flows from transactional banking and wealth management. For SCBK, the challenge is balancing a deposit franchise that remains attractive to customers with disciplined lending that preserves a healthy NIM, all while maintaining a payout policy that is sustainable under potential stress scenarios. Watch for any shifts in the monetary policy stance, changes in the frequency or size of government security auctions, and any statements about liquidity conditions or regulatory capital planning.
