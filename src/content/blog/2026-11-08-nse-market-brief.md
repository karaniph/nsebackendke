## Risks

If AIB’s debt-issuance trajectory shifts with stronger or weaker demand, expect a quick repricing of short-dated bonds and bank equities. External funding costs, especially for domestic names with offshore funding implications, remain a potential pressure point should risk appetite waver on an external shock.
