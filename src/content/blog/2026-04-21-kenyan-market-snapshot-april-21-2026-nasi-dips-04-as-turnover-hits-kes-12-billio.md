Market pulse

Equity markets opened cautiously, with the NASI slipping to 189.12 at the bell before extending losses to 188.73 by midday. Turnover contracted to KES 1.2 billion, reflecting a broad pullback after Monday's KES 1.4 billion session. Foreign investors were net sellers, offloading KES 340 million in blue-chip counters, while domestic institutional buyers offered intermittent support in mid-cap names.

Trading patterns pointed to rotation into defensive sectors. Industrials volume ran 22% above the 30-day average, driven by sustained demand in construction-linked stocks. Banking was mixed — Equity Group ticked up on bargain hunting while KCB Group slipped on thin liquidity.
