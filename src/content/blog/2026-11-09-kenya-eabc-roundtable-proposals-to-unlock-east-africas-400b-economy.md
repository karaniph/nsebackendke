Nairobi — 9 Nov 2026

## What happened

- Movers: The East Africa Business Council (EABC) hosted a CEOs' roundtable in Kenya in August 2026 that outlined proposals to unlock East Africa's $400 billion economy and boost regional trade and investment ties. This framing emphasizes turning policy talk into concrete investment-led actions to spur cross-border capital and development projects (source: https://eabc-online.com/eabc-ceos-roundtable-in-kenya-outlines-proposals-to-unlock-east-africas-400-billion-economy-and-boost-regional-trade-and-investment-ties/).
- Movers: The East African press has also highlighted a parallel move by British International Investment, which is shifting its East Africa strategy toward mobilizing local capital, including domestic pension funds, to back growth (source: https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business-tech/bii-shifts-east-africa-strategy-eyes-local-capital-5661752).

## Why it matters for NSE investors

- If the roundtable proposals translate into policy and regulatory steps, the turnaround could improve cross-border trade and investment conditions in East Africa. That creates potential revenue and margins upside for NSE-listed firms with exposure to East African markets and value chains (source: https://eabc-online.com/eabc-ceos-roundtable-in-kenya-outlines-proposals-to-unlock-east-africas-400-billion-economy-and-boost-regional-trade-and-investment-ties/).
- The BII move to mobilize local capital signals a broader push to deepen domestic funding channels for East Africa. For investors, that could affect the availability and cost of capital for regional projects and companies, potentially impacting equity and debt dynamics in the region (source: https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business-tech/bii-shifts-east-africa-strategy-eyes-local-capital-5661752).

- Risks:
  - Policy rollout risk: if proposals stall in government or regulatory processes, the anticipated uplift in regional investment may not materialize quickly.   - Funding channel risk: mobilizing local capital at scale can face regulatory hurdles and market frictions, which could delay impact on project financing. (source: https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business-tech/bii-shifts-east-africa-strategy-eyes-local-capital-5661752).
